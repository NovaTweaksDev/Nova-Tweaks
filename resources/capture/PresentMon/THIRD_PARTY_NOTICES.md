# PresentMon

Nova Tweaks redistributes the official Intel/GameTechDev PresentMon 2.5.1 x64
console executable and the official 1.10.0 x64 console executable as a legacy
compatibility fallback. PresentMon is licensed under the MIT License.

- Official project: https://github.com/GameTechDev/PresentMon
- Releases: https://github.com/GameTechDev/PresentMon/releases
- Complete Nova Tweaks provenance and hash record:
  `third-party-components.json` at the repository root

The unmodified upstream `LICENSE.txt` and `THIRD_PARTY.txt` files are preserved
beside the executables and under `licenses/PresentMon`.
